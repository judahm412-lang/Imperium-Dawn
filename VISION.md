# Imperium Dawn

On a tidally locked world, civilization survives in the narrow twilight belt between a burning day and a frozen night. You lead a young empire rebuilding around ancient solar engines. Expansion means choosing where your people can thrive and who controls the routes between surviving settlements.

Core fantasy: turn a fragile frontier settlement into a civilization that can shape its world's future.

Distinctive direction: heat, energy networks, and competing interpretations of ancient technology drive geography, trade, and diplomacy.

## Three pillars
1. Geography creates choices: terrain and climate shape settlement and conflict.
2. Empires tell stories: readable consequences, distinct rivals, and negotiated power.
3. Every system is inspectable: deterministic simulation, clear forecasts, and useful logs.

M1 establishes a navigable terrain map; climate and energy networks are later systems, not present mechanics.
